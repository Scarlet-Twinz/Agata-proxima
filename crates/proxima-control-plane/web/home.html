WHY PROXIMA

The security model is part of the product.

The control plane manages intent and evidence. The Engine remains authoritative for data-plane enforcement, even when management services are unavailable.

01 · ENGINE

Enforce at the boundary

Tenant context and session identity are established before normal database traffic reaches PostgreSQL.

02 · VERIFY

Prove the boundary

Isolation, identity, RLS and failure behavior are verified as evidence instead of assumed from UI state.

03 · CLOUD

Operate the fleet

Manage organizations, tenants, policies, nodes, deployments, billing and audit without making cloud availability the enforcement dependency.

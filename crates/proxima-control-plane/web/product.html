PRODUCT

The infrastructure boundary for tenant isolation.

Agata Proxima combines an enforcement engine with a non-authoritative control plane so teams can manage identity, policies, fleets and evidence without making management availability part of the runtime security decision.

CORE PRODUCT

Four connected capabilities.

01 · Enforcement Engine

Rust-based runtime enforcement around tenant context, sessions, TLS and PostgreSQL access.

02 · Control Plane

Organization, tenant, policy, fleet and deployment management with explicit desired versus observed state.

03 · Verification

Security checks become recorded evidence. The platform distinguishes evidence from a decorative status indicator.

04 · Audit

Administrative and security activity is retained as organization-scoped audit history.

05 · Commercial lifecycle

Plans, Stripe checkout, entitlements and billing state are handled server-side rather than trusted to frontend state.

06 · Operations

Fleet enrollment, deployments, support requests and infrastructure posture live beside the security boundary.

WHY TWO PLANES?

Management is not enforcement.

Control Plane

Stores desired state and manages organizations, policies, nodes, deployments, evidence, billing and support.

Its outage must never become an authorization decision.

Data Plane

The Proxima Engine remains responsible for the runtime path: tenant context, session binding and database enforcement.

Existing engine enforcement continues independently of management availability.
